Making this blog

Posted on October 19, 2025 · Category: Personal

I always feel I have a lot of things to say and not enough to say it to. I am starting this blog for this exact purpose. Here I will write what I hope this blog will be. In terms of hobbies, I am a big fan of music and literature, I enjoy talking about this media, and would love to talk about what I'm reading or listening to here. Technically, it is always fun to reflect on what is going on in my classes and projects I work on. I am always hectically working on stuff so getting a reset to document my progress is always fun. Personally, reflecting on life and everything else that goes with it is really fun. TLDR; I hope to use this space to reflect on my life, both for others to experience with me and for myself to look back on!
